About
Sooa Mo

Graduated from Simon Fraser University's Interactive Arts & Technology program, I design intuitive, engaging digital experiences rooted in storytelling and prototyping. I'm drawn to work that connects people or addresses real-world challenges.

This portfolio was originally hand-coded in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — later updated and maintained with the help of Claude AI. Deployed via GitHub Pages.
